Chicken Biryani

پاکستانی چکن بریانی

Category: Pakistani | Time: 45 minutes

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • Chicken - 500g
  • Rice - 2 cups
  • Onions - 2
  • Tomatoes - 2
  • Yogurt and biryani masala

Preparation

  1. Cook onions, tomatoes and chicken with spices.
  2. Boil rice separately.
  3. Make layers of chicken and rice.
  4. Cook on low heat for 15 minutes.

Preparation
